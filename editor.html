Map from screenshots

  1. On your server, open the map and hide all markers. Zoom in until town names and roads are clear, then don't change the zoom again.
  2. Start at the top-left corner of the map. Take a screenshot (F12 in Steam, or Print Screen).
  3. Drag the map so about a third of the last shot still shows, and take the next one. Go left to right across the top row.
  4. Move down a row (again keeping about a third overlapping), go back to the left edge, and repeat until you reach the bottom-right corner.
  5. Drop all the screenshots below. They're put in order by file name, which is the order you took them in.

Drag a box over the map area of this screenshot, leaving out the game's menus and edges. The same box is used for every screenshot.

Drag a box exactly around the map's edges to trim off anything outside the map, then press Use this map.
